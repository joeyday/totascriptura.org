Scripture prooftexts for each question of the Westminster Shorter Catechism—a superset of the Westminster Assembly’s own proofs (1648) and those in the OPC and PCA editions.

## Introduction

Q1. What is the chief end of man?
: Ps 16:5–11; 73:25–28; 86:9; 144:15; Isa 12:2; 60:21; Lk 2:10; Ro 11:36; 1Co 6:20; 10:31; Php 4:4; Rev 4:11; 21:3–4

Q2. What rule hath God given to direct us how we may glorify and enjoy him?
: Ge 2:24; Dt 4:2; Ps 19:7–11; Isa 8:20; Mt 19:4–5; Lk 24:27, 44; Jn 15:11; 20:30–31; Ac 17:11; 1Co 2:13; 14:37; Eph 2:20; 2Ti 3:15–17; 2Pe 1:20–21; 3:2, 15–16; 1Jn 1:3–4

Q3. What do the Scriptures principally teach?
: Ge 1:1; Dt 10:12–13; Jos 1:8; Ps 119:105; Mic 6:8; Jn 5:39; 20:31; Ro 10:17; 2Ti 1:13; 3:15–17

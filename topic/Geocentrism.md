This topic collects Bible verses that could be used to support geocentrism, flat earthism, and similar ideas out of step with modern science.

Sun and planets move through sky
: Ex 17:12; Jos 10:12–13; Ps 19:4–6; Ecc 1:5; Isa 38:8–9; Hab 3:11

Sky is a rigid dome (or “firmament”)
: Ge 1:6–8, 14–15, 17, 20; Job 37:18; Ps 19:1; 150:1; Pr 8:28; Eze 1:22–23, 25–26; 10:1; Da 12:3

Windows in firmament let rain through
: Ge 7:11; 8:2

Earth is flat (a circle or a square?)
: 1Sa 2:8; Job 26:10; Job 38:13 NIV; Pr 8:27–29; Isa 11:12; 40:22; 41:9; Eze 7:2; Rev 7:1; 20:8

Earth doesn’t move
: 1Ch 16:30; Ps 93:1; 96:10; 104:5; Isa 14:7

Value of π is 3
: 1Ki 7:23 (cf. 2Ch 4:2)
